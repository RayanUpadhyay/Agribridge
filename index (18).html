What it does

Connecting farmers to smarter agriculture.

Built for farmers earning under ₹10,000 a month, for whom the right information, in the right language, can change a season.

🏪

Markets

Local mandis near you: what they buy and sell, their hours and how big they are, so you know where to get the best price.

📜

Schemes

Government schemes like PM-KISAN, with who qualifies, how often it pays and a link to apply.

🏦

Loans

Farm credit like the Kisan Credit Card, with the interest rate and the maximum amount up front.

☀️

Subsidies

Help with equipment like solar pumps under PM-KUSUM: how much of the cost is covered, who’s eligible and the deadline.

💬

Chatbot

The AgriBridge Assistant answers farming questions in your own language.

How it works

Three steps. No English required.

Install it

Download the app below onto any Android phone. It’s free.

Pick your language

English, हिन्दी, मराठी or ಕನ್ನಡ. Everything follows your choice.

Find what you need

Browse markets, schemes, loans and subsidies, or just ask the chatbot.

The journey

It started by listening to farmers.

Early results suggest around a 10% rise in income for the farmers using it.

  1. Listening firstTalking with farmers to understand what they actually needed: markets, schemes and credit, in their own language.
  2. Raising ₹1,23,000A fundraiser paid for building the app and its chatbot.
  3. Validating itFeatures checked with NABARD, Farmer Producer Organisations and Seva Kendras.
  4. Testing in the fieldNow being tested with 100 farmers in four languages.
  5. New YorkPresented at the 64th UN Commission for Social Development as a 1M1B Changemaker.
