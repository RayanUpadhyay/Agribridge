# agribridge.rayanupadhyay.com

The home page for AgriBridge, my Android app that helps farmers find nearby markets, government schemes, loans and subsidies in their own language. Built in collaboration with [1M1B](https://www.1m1b.com).

**Live:** [agribridge.rayanupadhyay.com](https://agribridge.rayanupadhyay.com)

## Features

- A hero tagline that cycles through all four of the app's languages: English, हिन्दी, मराठी and ಕನ್ನಡ
- A phone mockup of the app's real home screen, rebuilt from the app's own layouts, colours, icons and photos
- What the app does: markets, government schemes, loans, subsidies and the AgriBridge Assistant chatbot
- How it works, in three steps
- The story so far: validated with NABARD, Farmer Producer Organisations and Seva Kendras, ₹1,23,000 raised, around 100 farmers testing it, and a presentation at the 64th UN Commission for Social Development as a 1M1B Changemaker
- An "In collaboration with 1M1B" badge in the hero
- A link to [@project_agribridge](https://www.instagram.com/project_agribridge/) on Instagram
- A download card for the APK (version 1.0, 11.9 MB) with its SHA-256 checksum, plus a notice for visitors who aren't on Android
- Light and dark mode, and a layout that works on phones

## Tech

A single `index.html` with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and no build step. Fonts come from Google Fonts. The AgriBridge logo, the 1M1B logo and the mockup's photos and icons are taken from the app itself and embedded in the page, so there are no other files to upload. The APK itself is hosted on the main site at [rayanupadhyay.com/downloads/agribridge-1.0.apk](https://rayanupadhyay.com/downloads/agribridge-1.0.apk), so this repo stays small. Hosted on Vercel.
