# agribridge.rayanupadhyay.com

The home page for AgriBridge, my Android app that helps farmers find nearby markets, government schemes and credit information in their own language.

**Live:** [agribridge.rayanupadhyay.com](https://agribridge.rayanupadhyay.com)

## Features

- A hero tagline that cycles through all four of the app's languages: English, हिन्दी, मराठी and ಕನ್ನಡ
- What the app does: nearby markets, government schemes, credit information and a chatbot for quick questions
- How it works, in three steps
- The story so far: validated with NABARD, Farmer Producer Organisations and Seva Kendras, ₹1,23,000 raised, around 100 farmers testing it, and a presentation at the 64th UN Commission for Social Development as a 1M1B Changemaker
- A link to [@project_agribridge](https://www.instagram.com/project_agribridge/) on Instagram
- A download card for the APK (version 1.0, 11.9 MB) with its SHA-256 checksum, plus a notice for visitors who aren't on Android
- Light and dark mode, and a layout that works on phones

## Tech

A single `index.html` with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and no build step. Fonts come from Google Fonts. The phone mockup is pure CSS. The APK itself is hosted on the main site at [rayanupadhyay.com/downloads/agribridge-1.0.apk](https://rayanupadhyay.com/downloads/agribridge-1.0.apk), so this repo stays small. Hosted on Vercel.
